The firm

A boutique firm built on relationships.

Nevajen Capital was founded in London in 2023. What we offer is access. Our partners know the people who buy businesses, fund them and invest in them, and we introduce you to the right ones directly. The partner you speak to at the start stays with the mandate until it completes.

Our partners come from investment banking, private equity, venture capital, commodities and diplomacy. On cross border transactions this network matters most, because the hardest part is rarely the analysis. It is finding the right counterparty and keeping both sides engaged through due diligence.

Services

Introductions come first. Everything needed to complete the deal follows.

Most mandates start with an introduction and add other services as the transaction develops. We agree the scope with you in writing before work begins.

  • Introductions

    Connecting the right parties

    This is the core of our work. We introduce buyers to sellers, companies to lenders and investors to opportunities that match their criteria. Every introduction is tracked and you receive regular written updates.

    • Buyers and sellers
    • Lenders and investors
    • Strategic partners

  • M&A

    Mergers and acquisitions advisory

    Support for buyers and sellers once the right counterparty is found. We prepare valuation analysis and financial models, coordinate due diligence materials and support negotiations up to signing.

    • Valuation and financial modelling
    • Negotiation support
    • Deal timetable

  • Capital

    Debt and equity structuring

    We help you decide how a transaction should be funded, whether through senior debt, mezzanine, convertibles or equity, and how the terms and covenants fit your timetable. We then introduce suitable lenders and investors.

    • Senior and mezzanine debt
    • Convertibles and equity
    • Terms and covenant review

  • Diligence

    Due diligence coordination and risk review

    Commercial, financial and operational review. We keep a log of the issues found, set out how each one can be addressed and summarise the findings for an investment committee.

    • Issue logging
    • Mitigation plans
    • Committee summaries

  • Execution

    Transaction support

    Practical support that keeps a transaction moving, including data room setup, information memoranda and coordination of transaction documents, so that the business is presented accurately.

    • Data room
    • Information memorandum
    • Document coordination

How a mandate runs

Five stages, with a timetable agreed at the start.

  1. 01

    Initial conversation

    Send us a short summary of the business and your objective. A managing partner reviews it and tells you directly whether we are the right fit.

  2. 02

    Scoping and preparation

    We agree the scope in writing, then prepare the financial model, materials and data room before any counterparty is approached.

  3. 03

    Targeted introductions

    We introduce you to a short list of buyers, targets, lenders or investors chosen for strategic fit and appetite. We do not run mass mailings.

  4. 04

    Diligence and negotiation

    We manage the process, hold both sides to the timetable and track every open point until it is resolved.

  5. 05

    Completion

    Documents are agreed, conditions are met and funds are transferred. We stay involved until closing.

FAQ

Questions we are asked most often.

How do introductions work?

You tell us what you are looking for, whether a buyer, a target, a lender or an investor. We approach a short list of parties that fit, introduce you once they are interested, and stay involved while the discussions move forward.

How do you help with funding for an acquisition?

We assess how the transaction should be funded, then introduce lenders and investors whose criteria fit it. We manage the discussions and keep them to the agreed timetable until terms are settled.

What types of funding do you work on?

Debt, equity and structured capital, often in combination. The right mix depends on what the funds are for, the covenants the business can carry and the timetable.

Do you act for buyers or sellers?

Both. We support buyers and sellers from first contact through to completion, with clear steps and documentation at each stage.

How long does a transaction take?

A straightforward funding can complete within a few weeks. Cross border or multi party transactions take longer. We give you a realistic timetable at the start and report against it.

Is my project suitable?

We work across sectors and deal sizes. Send a short summary of the business and your objective, and we will tell you quickly whether we can help.
